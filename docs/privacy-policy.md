# Privacy Policy for Nereus Anchor Watch

*Last updated: 16 October 2026*

Nereus Anchor Watch ("the app") helps you watch your boat's anchor using your iPhone's GPS. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes.

## Information on your devices

- **Your position, anchor location, alarm circle, track and settings** are stored on your devices. A paired Apple Watch receives monitoring information, and enabled Live Activities show monitoring information on system surfaces. Device backups may include saved app data. The app's developer cannot see them.
- The app has no account, no login and no advertising. It does not track you across other apps or websites.

## Information sent to other services

To show weather, wind, tides and alerts for where you are, the app sends your position (latitude and longitude, which may be precise) to these public services automatically once a location is available, and refreshes them while the app is running:

- **Open-Meteo** (open-meteo.com), for wind forecasts at your position and across the part of the chart you are viewing.
- **National Weather Service** (weather.gov), for weather alerts in the United States.
- **Map and chart providers** (OpenStreetMap, OpenSeaMap, NOAA and Apple Maps), which receive requests for the map area you are looking at so they can send the map pictures.

Tide and weather-station lookups (NOAA, the Canadian Hydrographic Service, Environment and Climate Change Canada) download public lists of stations and choose the nearest one on your phone, so your position is not sent for those.

When a stay is logged after raising the anchor, Apple’s reverse-geocoding service receives the anchor coordinates to suggest a place name.

These services may keep ordinary server logs under their own policies. The app's developer does not receive or store this information, and it is not linked to your name or identity.

## Notifications and background use

If you allow it, the app uses your location in the background and plays an alarm sound so it can keep watching your anchor with the screen off. You can turn this off at any time in the iPhone's Settings.

## Your choices

You can deny or withdraw location, notification and Live Activity permissions in the iPhone's Settings, or delete the app to remove its local data. Previously created device backups and third-party server logs are governed by their respective retention policies.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.
